# Detached recurrent-state region audit

The visually detached yellow region is defined here only for audit purposes as
samples with mean catalogue uncertainty >= 10 km. This threshold identifies a
physical recovery regime; it is not a cluster label inferred from UMAP.

It contains 1,464 of 16,872 representation samples (8.68%).

The accompanying CSV reports its scenario, time, episode, and sensor composition.
If it spans all sensors and is concentrated at early times, the separated region
should be interpreted as the recurrent core encoding the shared catalogue-recovery
transient, rather than a sensor-specific or isolated policy failure.
